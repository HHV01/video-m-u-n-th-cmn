## Prompt thumbnail hiện hành

Chừa vùng trống cho chữ ở các vị trí tương ứng. Thêm chữ lúc dựng, tối đa 5 từ. Giữ đen trắng và tối đa 3 nhân vật.

### Vấn đề → Giải pháp

```text
High-contrast black and white stick figure YouTube thumbnail, clean white background. Confused [CHARACTER A] on the left, confident [CHARACTER B] on the right, with a simple arrow between them. Leave blank space in the center for the headline and along the bottom for the situation. Keep reserved areas empty. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks.
--seed 88888 --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6
```

### So sánh

```text
High-contrast black and white stick figure YouTube thumbnail, clean white background. Place [CHARACTER A] and [CHARACTER B] on opposite sides. Reserve blank spaces above both figures for role labels, in the center for the comparison label, and along the bottom for the situation. Keep reserved areas empty. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks.
--seed 88888 --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6
```

### Công thức

```text
High-contrast black and white stick figure YouTube thumbnail, clean white background. Place [CHARACTER] on the left 40%. Reserve a large blank area on the right for the formula and a small blank strip at the bottom for the situation. Put [ICON] beside the blank area. Keep reserved areas empty. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks.
--seed 88888 --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6
```

## Bản gốc

## 3E. Thumbnail (3 mẫu)
Tham số: `--seed 88888 --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6`. Quy tắc: đen trắng, ≤ 3 nhân vật, ≤ 5 từ. Chữ nên thêm lại ở CapCut/Canva cho sắc nét.

**Mẫu 1 – Vấn đề → Giải pháp (Short)**
```text
youtube thumbnail composition, minimalist black and white stick figure style, clean white background with 40% negative space, bold typography, high contrast, split composition, left side: [CHARACTER A] with confused or frustrated expression, right side: [CHARACTER B] with happy confident expression, center: large bold text "[KEYWORD]", simple arrow from left character to right character, small bottom text "[SITUATION]"
Negative: color, gradient, realistic elements, clutter, watermark
--seed 88888 --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6
```

**Mẫu 2 – So sánh (video dài)**
```text
youtube thumbnail composition, minimalist black and white stick figure style, clean white background with 40% negative space, bold typography, high contrast, split composition, left: [CHARACTER A] with [EXPRESSION], label above "[ROLE A]", right: [CHARACTER B] with [EXPRESSION], label above "[ROLE B]", center: large bold "VS", small bottom text "[SITUATION]"
Negative: color, gradient, realistic elements, clutter, watermark
--seed 88888 --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6
```

**Mẫu 3 – Công thức**
```text
youtube thumbnail composition, minimalist black and white stick figure style, clean white background with 40% negative space, bold typography, high contrast, left-right composition, left 40%: [CHARACTER] with thinking or confident expression, right 60%: large text box with formula "[FORMULA]" where [X] and [Y] sit in small square outlines, small icon [ICON] next to formula, small bottom text "[SITUATION]"
Negative: color, gradient, realistic elements, clutter, watermark
--seed 88888 --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6
```

**Ví dụ:** Minh (frustrated) ← BLOCKED → Linh (happy), dưới: Daily Stand-up.
